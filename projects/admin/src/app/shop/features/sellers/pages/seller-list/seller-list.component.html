Seller Payouts
Seller commission
The shop keeps a % of every product a seller sells. Sellers with their own rate (set in Edit seller) use that; everyone else uses the default below.
%
1
Per product line. Commission is worked out on each product in an order: price × quantity (GST included, before any coupon — coupons are paid by the shop).
2
Which rate. The seller's own rate if set, otherwise the default rate.
3
Several products. An order with many products is just many lines. Each line uses the rate of the seller who sold it; the order's commission is the sum.
4
Locked per order. The rate is saved on each order line when the order is placed. Changing a rate later only affects new orders.
5
Payouts. Seller earning = line total − commission. It becomes payable once the order is delivered; returned items are taken off.
